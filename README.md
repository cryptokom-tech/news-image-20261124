entered in the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124)
"Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" ($100, top 50 paid).

## The image

A front page, **THE WORLD TODAY**: masthead and red date banner, a dotted
world map with numbered pins, and 36 numbered stories in three columns.

The lead story is Trump's claim that he "convinced" Nvidia to bring back
Jensen Huang as chief executive, with Wall Street's sharp rebound alongside
it. Around them: Tokyo's reopening after its holiday, North Korea weighing
the closure of Switzerland's Pyongyang office, the Canada–Qatar investment
pact due today, the CAS ruling on Senegal's Africa Cup appeal, Australia
naming AI a foreign-policy challenge, FIFA confirming the 2031 and 2035
Women's World Cup hosts, the Grok child-safety lawsuits, and the night's
Champions League fixtures — plus the rest of the day's world news, from Kyiv
to Wellington.

Every story was first reported on 24 November 2026 in some timezone, or is
scheduled for that day. Sources: Reuters, BBC, Associated Press.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) is the finished front page, rendered
locally and committed here. `main.py` uses only the Python standard library:
it validates the PNG signature and size, then copies `entry.png` byte for
byte to `/output/entry.png`. Running it twice writes identical bytes.

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.png
```
